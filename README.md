> A curated list of resources, frameworks, checklists, and methodologies for Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) flight test planning.

UAV Flight Test Planning is critical for ensuring safety, reliability, and regulatory compliance in unmanned aircraft system (UAS) operations. This list is intended for aerospace engineers, drone developers, researchers, and hobbyists involved in planning and executing UAV flight tests.

## Guidelines & Standards

- [ASTM F3266 - Standard Guide for Flight Test of Unmanned Aircraft Systems](https://www.astm.org/f3266-18.html) — Industry guide covering the planning and execution of UAS flight testing.
- [ICAO Manual on Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems (RPAS)](https://store.icao.int/en/manual-on-remotely-piloted-aircraft-systems-rpas-doc-10019) — International guidance covering RPAS airworthiness, operations, command and control, detect and avoid, and safety management.
- [EASA Easy Access Rules for Unmanned Aircraft Systems](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/easy-access-rules-unmanned-aircraft-systems) — Consolidated European UAS regulations, acceptable means of compliance, and guidance material.
- [FAA UAS Research, Development, Demonstration, and Testing](https://www.faa.gov/uas/research_development) — FAA resources covering UAS flight testing, modeling and simulation, risk assessment, and technology evaluation.
- [Flight Test Safety Committee](https://www.flighttestsafety.org/) — Professional organization promoting flight-test safety, education, and best practices.

## Flight Test Planning

- [FAA UAS Test Site Program](https://www.faa.gov/uas/programs_partnerships/test_sites) — FAA-designated facilities supporting UAS research, operational validation, safety cases, and flight testing.
- [FAA UAS Test Site Locations](https://www.faa.gov/uas/programs_partnerships/test_sites/locations) — Directory of FAA-designated UAS test sites and their testing capabilities.
- [Transport Canada Flight Reviewer’s Guide for RPAS](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/publications/flight-reviewer-s-guide-pilots-remotely-piloted-aircraft-systems) — Practical guidance covering pre-flight planning, site surveys, emergency procedures, aircraft preparation, and flight exercises.
- [FAA UAS Traffic Management Field Test](https://www.faa.gov/uas/research_development/traffic_management/field_test) — Example of structured UAS field testing involving complex operations and UAS traffic management capabilities.

## Methodologies & Frameworks

- [Systematic Flight Test Planning Framework (SFTPF)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8390592) — Structured methodology for planning UAV flight tests.
- [Specific Operations Risk Assessment (SORA)](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/easy-access-rules-unmanned-aircraft-systems) — Risk-based methodology for evaluating UAS operations and determining appropriate mitigations and assurance requirements.
- [FAA UAS Research and Development](https://www.faa.gov/uas/research_development) — Research framework encompassing flight testing, simulation, technology evaluation, risk assessment, and data analysis.
- [Flight Test Safety Committee](https://www.flighttestsafety.org/) — Resources and professional knowledge related to flight-test risk management and safety.

## Risk Assessment & Safety

- [EASA Specific Operations Risk Assessment (SORA)](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/online-publications/easy-access-rules-unmanned-aircraft-systems?erules-id=ERULES-1963177438-24086) — Structured methodology for assessing ground and air risks associated with UAS operations.
- [Transport Canada Standard 922 - RPAS Safety Assurance](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-922-rpas-safety-assurance) — Technical safety-assurance requirements for RPAS used in specified Canadian operations.
- [FAA UAS Research, Development, Demonstration, and Testing](https://www.faa.gov/uas/research_development) — Research resources addressing system safety, human factors, detect and avoid, communications, and certification.
- [ICAO Manual on Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems](https://store.icao.int/en/manual-on-remotely-piloted-aircraft-systems-rpas-doc-10019) — International reference covering RPAS safety management and operational integration.

## Pre-Flight Planning & Checklists

- [Transport Canada Flight Reviewer’s Guide for RPAS](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/publications/flight-reviewer-s-guide-pilots-remotely-piloted-aircraft-systems) — Includes practical criteria for site surveys, pre-flight planning, inspections, emergency procedures, and operational preparation.
- [UAV Coach Quadcopter Guide](https://uavcoach.com/how-to-fly-a-quadcopter-guide/) — Introductory resource covering pre-flight preparation and basic UAV operations.
- [DIY Drones](https://diydrones.com/) — Community resource containing UAV operational discussions, procedures, and historical checklist material.

## Simulation & Test Preparation

- [PX4 Simulation](https://docs.px4.io/main/en/simulation/) — Simulation resources for testing PX4-based aircraft and flight-control configurations before physical flight.
- [PX4 Hardware-in-the-Loop Simulation](https://docs.px4.io/main/en/simulation/hitl) — Guidance for testing PX4 firmware using actual flight-controller hardware connected to a simulated aircraft.
- [ArduPilot Simulation](https://ardupilot.org/dev/docs/simulation-1.html) — Simulation resources for testing ArduPilot vehicles, missions, parameters, and control logic.
- [ArduPilot SITL](https://ardupilot.org/dev/docs/sitl-simulator-software-in-the-loop.html) — Software-in-the-loop environment for testing ArduPilot without requiring a physical aircraft.

## Flight Control & Ground Station Tools

- [QGroundControl](https://qgroundcontrol.com/) — Ground control station supporting mission planning, vehicle configuration, telemetry, and flight monitoring.
- [Mission Planner](https://ardupilot.org/planner/) — Ground control station for planning, configuring, monitoring, and analyzing ArduPilot-based UAV operations.
- [MAVProxy](https://ardupilot.org/mavproxy/) — Command-line ground control station and developer tool for MAVLink-based UAV systems.
- [MAVLink](https://mavlink.io/) — Lightweight messaging protocol widely used for communication between UAVs, ground stations, and onboard components.

## Test Instrumentation & Data Collection

- [PX4 Flight Logging](https://docs.px4.io/main/en/dev_log/logging.html) — Documentation for configuring and collecting PX4 flight logs.
- [ArduPilot DataFlash Logs](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/common-downloading-and-analyzing-data-logs-in-mission-planner.html) — Guidance for downloading and analyzing detailed flight-controller logs.
- [MAVLink Telemetry](https://mavlink.io/en/services/) — MAVLink services useful for telemetry, mission management, parameters, commands, and other test data.

## Post-Flight Analysis

- [PX4 Flight Review](https://review.px4.io/) — Web-based visualization and analysis tool for PX4 flight logs.
- [PX4 Flight Review Log Analysis Guide](https://docs.px4.io/main/en/log/flight_review) — Guidance for interpreting PX4 Flight Review plots and identifying potential vehicle or control-system issues.
- [ArduPilot Log Analysis](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/common-downloading-and-analyzing-data-logs-in-mission-planner.html) — Documentation for examining flight logs using Mission Planner.
- [PlotJuggler](https://github.com/facontidavide/PlotJuggler) — Time-series visualization tool useful for inspecting telemetry and flight-test datasets.

## Regulatory Resources

### United States

- [FAA Small UAS Regulations - Part 107](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/small-unmanned-aircraft-systems-uas-regulations-part-107) — Core U.S. operating requirements for small UAS.
- [FAA Part 107 Airspace Authorizations](https://www.faa.gov/uas/commercial_operators/part_107_airspace_authorizations) — Guidance for obtaining authorization to conduct UAS operations in controlled airspace.
- [FAA UAS Test Site Program](https://www.faa.gov/uas/programs_partnerships/test_sites) — Test infrastructure and regulatory support for advanced UAS research and flight testing.

### Canada

- [Transport Canada Drone Safety](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/drone-safety) — Central portal for Canadian RPAS regulations, certification, operations, and safety guidance.
- [Transport Canada Flight Reviewer’s Guide for RPAS](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/publications/flight-reviewer-s-guide-pilots-remotely-piloted-aircraft-systems) — Operational and flight-planning guidance for Canadian RPAS flight reviews.
- [Transport Canada Standard 922 - RPAS Safety Assurance](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-922-rpas-safety-assurance) — Technical requirements supporting safety-assurance declarations for RPAS.

### European Union

- [EASA Civil Drones](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/civil-drones-rpas) — European regulatory information for UAS operations.
- [EASA Easy Access Rules for Unmanned Aircraft Systems](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/easy-access-rules-unmanned-aircraft-systems) — Consolidated European UAS regulations and guidance incorporating SORA.

### International

- [ICAO Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems](https://www.icao.int/safety/UA/Pages/Remotely-Piloted-Aircraft-Systems.aspx) — International resources supporting integration of remotely piloted aircraft into civil aviation.
- [ICAO Manual on Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems (Doc 10019)](https://store.icao.int/en/manual-on-remotely-piloted-aircraft-systems-rpas-doc-10019) — International reference for RPAS airworthiness, operations, C2, detect and avoid, and safety management.
